UK Payroll Processing, HMRC Statutory Compliance & General Ledger Portfolio

The repository provides a complete, audit-ready demonstration of end-to-end UK payroll management using Xero Payroll (with secondary cross-platform verification in QuickBooks Online). Designed as a comprehensive technical showcase, it spans operational gross-to-net pay runs for an 8-employee workforce, monthly employer tax reconciliation via P32 Employer Payment Summaries, year-end statutory compliance with P60 End-of-Year Certificates, and double-entry general ledger integration into the Profit & Loss statement and Balance Sheet.

All sensitive personal and corporate data within this portfolio has been fully redacted and anonymized under UK GDPR standards.

1. Executive Portfolio Summary

The project documents a complete payroll cycle for a UK-based haulage and logistics enterprise operating a weekly pay schedule. It demonstrates proficiency across all core stages of UK payroll management: initial employee setup, tax code application, workplace pension auto-enrolment, statutory RTI submission preparation, and accounting reconciliation.

Key Financial & Payroll Metrics (Tax Week Ending 07 March 2026)

2. Three-Tier Technical Architecture

To deliver clear auditing visibility for technical recruiters, accounting clients, and payroll managers, this repository is structured into three interconnected compliance tiers:

Tier	Primary Deliverables	Core Compliance & Accounting Functions
Tier 1: Weekly Operations	Xero Payslips, QBO Payslips, Payroll Activity Details	Gross-to-net pay calculations, hourly rate application, overtime tracking, NEST pension auto-enrolment, and tax code application (1257L, S1207L, K163).
Tier 2: Monthly Statutory Reporting	P32 Employer Payment Summaries (Tax Months 1–12)	Aggregation of PAYE Tax and Class 1 Primary/Secondary NIC liabilities, tracking Employment Allowance eligibility, and calculating monthly HMRC remittance obligations.
Tier 3: Year-End & General Ledger	P60 Certificates, P&L, Balance Sheet, Transaction Journal	Year-end tax certification for active staff, reconciliation of cumulative gross pay, double-entry payroll posting, and balance sheet liability accounting.

Technical Analysis of UK Tax Treatments Applied

1. Standard Personal Allowance (1257L): Applied to standard UK residents (e.g., J. Irving, J.G. Cook), granting an annual tax-free allowance of £12,570 (£242 weekly threshold).
2. Scottish Income Tax (S1207L): Applied to employee T.I. Duff, utilizing Scottish starter, basic, and intermediate tax bands rather than rest-of-UK tax brackets.
3. K-Code Negative Allowance (K163): Applied to employee I. Miller. K-codes indicate that untaxed benefits or prior-year adjustments exceed the annual personal allowance, adding £1,630 to taxable income and requiring additional PAYE withholding (subject to the 50% regulatory max cap).
4. Tax Refund Reconciliations: Employee Tony Robertshaw generated a negative PAYE tax deduction (-£126.17) due to cumulative tax recalculation, where prior YTD tax paid exceeded total revised tax liability.
5. Workplace Pension Auto-Enrolment (NEST): Qualified earnings calculated above the weekly threshold (£120/week), applying standard 5% employee and 3% employer contribution rates.
